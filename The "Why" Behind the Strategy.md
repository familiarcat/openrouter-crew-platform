The "Why" Behind the Strategy
Google Search Control: When a guest sees an "OpenTable" button on Google, it suggests Bar Italia is just another client of a conglomerate. By moving to a "Toast" button that matches our website's direct link, we signal a direct relationship.

Long-Time Customer Comfort: We avoid "flashy" marketing. Instead of "Sign up for 10% off," we use language like "Stay in touch for news on our wine dinners." It feels like a conversation, not a sales pitch.

Squarespace/Wix Selection: These tools are excellent for "Vibe Coding." They allow us to spend 80% of our time on the look and feel (the brand soul) and only 20% on the code, which fits the accelerated 4-week timeline perfectly.
